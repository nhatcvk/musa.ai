Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Musa is a self-hosted video studio that runs on its owner's own computer. This policy explains what it does with data from Google and YouTube when a YouTube channel is connected to it.

YouTube API Services

Musa uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own collection and use of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

What Musa accesses

  • Channel profile: the channel's id, name, handle and picture, to show which channel is connected.
  • Uploads: Musa uploads the videos, thumbnails and text (title, description, tags, visibility, audience and AI-content settings) that the owner chooses to publish, and keeps the resulting video id and link.
  • Channel management, when granted: reading and updating the owner's own videos, playlists, captions and comments at the owner's request.
  • Analytics, when granted: YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's own content, such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and, for monetized channels, estimated revenue.

How data is stored

  • All data stays on the computer where Musa runs, in its local database and files. Musa has no servers of its own and sends nothing to its developers.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key that only exists on that computer, and are never sent to a web browser.
  • Data from YouTube is only sent back to Google's own APIs, to perform the actions described above.

Sharing

Musa does not sell, share or transfer any user data, including data obtained through YouTube API Services, to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting a channel in Musa deletes its stored tokens and its post history from the local database. Videos already published on YouTube are not affected.
  • You can revoke Musa's access at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored analytics data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as the YouTube API Services policies require.

Cookies

Musa's local web interface uses cookies only to remember the interface language, the selected workspace and, for ten minutes, the state of a channel connection in progress.
