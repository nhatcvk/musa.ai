A personal AI video studio

Musa writes, produces and renders short videos, then publishes them to its owner's own YouTube channels.

What it does with YouTube. When the owner connects a channel, Musa uses the YouTube Data API to upload finished videos and Shorts with their title, description, tags, visibility and thumbnail, at a time the owner chooses. It can also read the channel's basic profile and its YouTube Analytics reports so the owner can see how videos perform.

Who uses it. Musa is a self-hosted tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service and does not offer accounts to other people.

Musa uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Read how Musa itself handles data in its Privacy Policy.
